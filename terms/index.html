1. What NabNear is

NabNear lets a driver share that a public street parking spot is about to open, and lets nearby drivers see it. NabNear is an information service only. It does not own, control, reserve or assign any parking space.

2. No guarantee

A shared spot may already be taken, may never open, or may be in the wrong place. NabNear does not guarantee that any spot is available, legal or safe to use. Alerts may be late or may not arrive.

3. No holding, reserving or selling spots

  • Do not wait in, hold or save a spot for a particular driver. When you share a spot, pull out as soon as it is safe.
  • Do not offer, ask for or accept money, tips or anything of value for a public street spot.
  • NabNear gives no one priority or a right to any spot. Parking and traffic rules still govern who may park there.

4. Drive safely and follow the law

  • Never use NabNear while driving, including at red lights or in traffic. Use it only when safely parked, or let a passenger use it.
  • You are responsible for obeying parking signs, meters, permits, street cleaning and all traffic laws. A spot shown in NabNear may be illegal to park in.

5. Honest reports

Share a spot only when you are actually about to leave it, and report outcomes truthfully. Do not post fake spots, spam, or try to disrupt the service. We may limit or block anyone who misuses NabNear.
