Support

Quick answers about how NabNear works, your privacy, and how to reach us.

Is NabNear free?

Yes. There are no fees, no ads and no paid features.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up. The first time you open the app it creates a random, anonymous account on its own. We never ask for your name, email or phone number, and other drivers never see your account.

When does NabNear use my location?

Only while the app is open, and it leaves your phone only when you tap Leaving now or Alert me here. Alert me at… uses the destination you choose, not where you are. NabNear never tracks you in the background.

Can I reserve or hold a spot?

No. NabNear never holds, reserves or sells a spot, and you should never wait in a spot for someone or accept money for one. A shared spot is a heads-up; normal parking rules decide who parks there.

The spot was gone when I arrived. What now?

It happens: another car may have taken it first. Tap Someone took it or No spot here so the spot closes for everyone else. Turn on an alert to hear about the next one.

How do alerts work?

Alert me here watches for spots opening within about 400 m of you for 15 minutes. Alert me at… watches around an address you pick, from about 10 minutes before you arrive until 10 minutes after. Tap Stop to end either one.

Is a spot on the map always legal to park in?

Not necessarily. Always check signs, meters, permits and street-cleaning times before you park. You’re responsible for following parking and traffic rules.

Where can I use NabNear?

NabNear is in private testing in Philadelphia, on Android first with iPhone to follow. Ask for early access.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my data. You can also enter your deletion code on the delete your data page. Either way, the account and everything linked to it is removed.

How do I report a problem?

In the app, use Report a problem; it opens an email with your app version and phone type filled in. Or write to hi@nabnear.com.

Still stuck?

Write to us and a person will reply, usually within two business days.
