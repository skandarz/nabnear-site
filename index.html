Stopcircling.

When a driver is about to leave a street spot, they tap once. When you’re nearby and looking, you hear about it. That’s NabNear: free, anonymous, and built for city blocks.

Request early accessSee how it works

Three taps between two drivers.

No bidding, no waiting in the street, no account to fill in. One driver shares, another finds, and the map keeps itself honest.

  1. Step 1

    Leaving?

    Still parked and about to go? Tap once before you pull out. Drivers nearby see a spot is opening.

  2. Step 2

    Looking?

    Turn on an alert and NabNear tells you when a spot is opening close to you, or close to where you’re headed.

  3. Step 3

    Nabbed it.

    Got the spot, or someone beat you to it? Say so with one tap, so the map stays honest for everyone.

Alert me here

Already circling? NabNear watches for spots opening within about 400 m of you for the next 15 minutes. Tap Stop any time.

Alert me at…

Heading somewhere? Pick the address and when you’ll arrive. Alerts start about 10 minutes before you get there, and NabNear never reads your current location for this.

The rules we built it on.

Street parking belongs to everyone. NabNear only passes along a heads-up, and these lines don’t move.

Never held, reserved or sold
Nobody waits in a spot for you and nobody pays for one. A shared spot is a heads-up, and the usual first-come rules still apply.
Anonymous by design
No name, email or phone number. The app creates a random account the first time you open it, and other drivers never see it.
Location only when you tap
Your location leaves your phone only when you tap Leaving now or Alert me here. No background tracking, ever.
Free
No fees, no ads, no data sold. Delete everything from the app or from this site whenever you like.

Starting on Philadelphia’s blocks.

Rowhouse streets, tight curbs and spots that open one at a time. NabNear is being tested here first, block by block, before it goes anywhere else.
