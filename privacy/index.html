Privacy Policy

What NabNear collects, why, how long we keep it, and your choices.

Privacy Policy

Version: Terms r4 / Privacy r7 · Effective November 23, 2026 · NabNear is in testing.

NabNear LLC ("NabNear", "we") runs the NabNear app, which lets drivers share that a street parking spot is about to open. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

No name, email or phone needed

You use NabNear without giving your name, email, phone number or contacts. The first time you open the app, our database provider creates an anonymous account for you: a random account ID on our servers, with a sign-in key saved on your phone. Other users are never sent your account ID.

Early access list (website only)

If you join the early access list on nabnear.com, we collect what you type in: your name, email, neighborhood and phone type. We use it only to invite you to the beta and tell you when it opens near you. It is stored in our Google Workspace account (Google Forms and Google Sheets), never sold or shared, and kept until the beta ends or you ask us to delete it by emailing hi@nabnear.com. The list is separate from the app: joining it does not link your email to your app account.

Location

NabNear uses your location only while the app is open on your screen, and never in the background. If you have already allowed location, the app uses it to center the map; that location stays on your phone. Your current location is sent to us only when you tap Leaving now or Alert me here. Alert me at… sends us only the destination you choose. While you type a destination, the letters you type and the center of the map on your screen go to Google Places to suggest matching places; your own location is not sent. If you tap Directions on a spot, the app opens the map app you choose (for example Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps) with that spot's location; that app's own privacy policy then applies, and NabNear receives nothing from it.

A spot you post is shown on the map to all NabNear users for up to 4 minutes, or until it closes. Drivers who tapped Heading there for it can still see that spot, to report what happened, until 15 minutes after it expires.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal information, or share it for advertising.
  • We do not include advertising or analytics tools in the app. Crash reports go to Sentry, named in the table below, and never include your location or account ID.
  • We keep your posts linked to your account ID for 90 days. We do not use them to profile you or to follow where you go.

Your choices and rights

  • Location permission is needed to post a spot or choose an alert location. Notification permission is needed to receive alerts. You can browse the map without either. Turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Stop alerts in the app with Stop. This deletes your alert location at once; your push token is kept so alerts work next time, until a deletion trigger above applies.
  • Delete your data in the app: Settings (☰), then Delete my data. This deletes your account ID and everything linked to it.
  • Deletion code: Settings shows a code for your install. Save it before you uninstall, because uninstalling does not tell us to delete anything. Keep it private: anyone with the code can ask us to see or delete that data. To delete after uninstalling, enter the code at nabnear.com/delete or email it to hi@nabnear.com; we delete that account's data within 30 days and ask for nothing else.
  • Ask what data we hold for your code: email hi@nabnear.com.

Children

NabNear is for drivers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Database rules let each person change only their own reports and hide other users' account IDs. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the version and date above and show a notice in the app before the change takes effect.
