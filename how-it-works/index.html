How it works

NabNear is a heads-up between two drivers: one leaving a street spot, one looking for one nearby. Here’s everything it does.

From one curb to the next.

  1. Step 1

    Leaving?

    Still parked and about to go? Tap once before you pull out. Drivers nearby see a spot is opening.

  2. Step 2

    Looking?

    Turn on an alert and NabNear tells you when a spot is opening close to you, or close to where you’re headed.

  3. Step 3

    Nabbed it.

    Got the spot, or someone beat you to it? Say so with one tap, so the map stays honest for everyone.

Alert me here

Already circling? NabNear watches for spots opening within about 400 m of you for the next 15 minutes. Tap Stop any time.

Alert me at…

Heading somewhere? Pick the address and when you’ll arrive. Alerts start about 10 minutes before you get there, and NabNear never reads your current location for this.

What other drivers see

When you tap Leaving now, the spot appears on the map for nearby drivers for up to 4 minutes, or until someone reports what happened. They see the spot, never who shared it.

Heading there

A driver who taps Heading there can open directions in the map app they already use, such as Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps. NabNear hands over the spot’s location and nothing else.

Keeping the map honest

After a spot is shared, drivers report what happened: I got it, Someone took it, or No spot here. Those reports close the spot quickly, so nobody chases a space that’s already gone.

What NabNear won’t do

It won’t hold a spot for you, sell one, or let anyone pay for priority. Parking signs, meters, permits and street cleaning rules still apply to every spot you see.
