About NabNear

We’re a small Philadelphia company working on an everyday city problem: the minutes, fuel and patience lost circling the block for a street spot.

Why we started

On most city blocks, someone is about to leave a spot at the same moment someone else is circling for one. The two never know about each other. NabNear connects them with a single tap, so the spot goes to a driver who needs it instead of sitting empty while cars loop past.

What we believe

Public street parking belongs to everyone. NabNear passes along a heads-up and nothing more: spots are never held, reserved or sold, and nobody gets priority. We ask for no name, email or phone number, use your location only when you tap, and sell no data.

Where we are

NabNear is in private testing in Philadelphia. We’re refining the app with a small group of drivers before opening it more widely, on Android first and iPhone next.

Get in touch

For early access, partnerships, press or anything else, write to hi@nabnear.com. A person reads every message.
